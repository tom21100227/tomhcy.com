Ever since I left school and the Microsoft ecosystem I've been on a journey to find a good way to manage my emails. Sounds simple but my situation is a bit complicated: 

# My status quo

I daily drive 5 inboxes with 4 accounts with different providers and rules: 
- Outlook: my main email, with an alias that forwards everything to my work inbox. 
- Gmail: A junk email where my Apple ID is attached to, get's all the purchase recipts and a bunch of marketing emails. 
- Gandi Email: Comes with my domain purchase from them, I've been slowly transitioning into using it, but it's not perfect yet and the web client that comes with it is not very user-friendly per-se. 
- iCloud : Personal email, don't really use it that often but all private-relay email are forwarded to this inbox, so everything with Sign-in with Apple is there, sometimes very useful.

I am also considering transitioning to Proton Mail for it's security and privacy features, but I'm not sure if it's the right thing to do yet, perhaps when my Gandi Inbox free period is over I will try that.

## Painpoint for now: 

1. I have 3 apps for emails, outlook, gmail, and iOS native app for gandi and iCloud. But Gandi Email never syncs up well because Apple Mail does not implement the IMAP IDLE command, which is essential for real-time email notifications with those custom domains.
